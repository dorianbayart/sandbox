Neural Lab

A genetic algorithm trains small neural networks to drive. Change the network size and compare.

Network

Extra outputs that come back as inputs at the next step: the network can keep something in mind from one step to the next. Nothing tells it what: evolution finds out, for example whether an obstacle gets closer (a speed), that it is overtaking, or how long it has been braking.

  • 0: the network only sees the present moment, like a photo.
  • 1 or 2: enough for a trend or a state. Each one adds a few parameters.

Inputs: the rays, the speed, the two cut-ins, the memory. Outputs: throttle, brake (stopped, holding it reverses), steering, then the memory.

Evolution

Each child copies its parents' parameters, then a few of them are changed at random. This sets how many, on average. The chance for each parameter to change (the mutation rate) follows from it, so the setting stays valid whatever the network size.

  • Too many (dozens): children no longer look like their parents, good brains get broken, the average stays low and the best curve jumps around.
  • Too few (under 1): most children are copies, progress is slow and the curve stays flat.
  • Start around 3 to 10.

A changed parameter moves by a random amount: usually less than this size, rarely up to 2 or 3 times more (a gaussian). Parameters themselves are mostly between −1 and 1.

  • Big (≥ 0.5): big jumps, good to explore at the start, but they spoil a brain that already drives well.
  • Small (≤ 0.1): fine tuning, good once the cars drive, too slow to find a first solution.
  • So it decreases at each generation, down to the minimum. Too fast, and evolution freezes before the cars drive well; the minimum keeps it going.
  • Fine-tune a champion: import it with a small size (0.1) and no decrease.

Champion

The car with the best validation score, as a JSON file with the network shape, its sensors and its parameters, to keep or reuse later.
Track: easy = wide, gentle curves; hard = tight curves, twisty stretches and a road that narrows and widens; progressive = easy, then medium from 3,000 px, hard from 8,000 px.
